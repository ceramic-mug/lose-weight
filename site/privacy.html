Privacy

Effective November 5, 2026

Short version: what you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't collect it, see it, sell it, or share it.

What the app stores

Everything you enter, kept to make the app work:

  • Your profile: sex, birth year, height, weight, goal, activity level and settings.
  • Meals (where from, how healthy, portion, time), drinks, workouts and weigh-ins.

This is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it also syncs through your private iCloud database so your iPhone and Mac stay in step. Apple holds that data under its own privacy policy. We can't access it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere. It doesn't contact any server of ours, because there isn't one.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the app:

  • reads your workouts and body weight, so you don't have to enter them twice, and
  • writes the weights you log back to Health.

Health data is only used inside the app. It is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never stored in iCloud by us. You can change access at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Your control

  • Export: Settings › Export as CSV gives you a copy of everything.
  • Edit or delete: any entry, any time, from Today or History.
  • Delete everything: Settings › Delete all data removes your data from the device and from iCloud. Data in Apple Health is not affected; manage it in the Health app.

Beta testing

If you test through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and basic usage information (like device model and OS version) with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. You can turn off sharing in the TestFlight app.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads a font from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services may log your IP address and browser details. See GitHub's and Google's privacy policies. The site uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

Children

The app is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
