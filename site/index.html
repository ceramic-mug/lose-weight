Questions

Do I have to count calories?

No. You tap where the food came from, how healthy it was, and how much. The app estimates a calorie range in the background. Calorie numbers are hidden unless you turn them on.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The app is built for manual entry. If you have a Watch or a smart scale, you can turn on Apple Health import in Settings.

How accurate are the food estimates?

They're honest ranges, not exact counts. The goal is to spot patterns, like how often you eat takeout, how big your portions are, and how many indulgent meals you have. That's where most of the gains are.

Is it safe for everyone?

It's designed for adults. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a history of an eating disorder, the app sets your budget to maintenance (no calorie cut) and suggests working with a clinician. It isn't medical advice.

Where's my data stored?

On your devices, synced privately through your own iCloud account. You can export everything as a CSV at any time.
